About this website

Taxi Roof Sign Ireland displays product information and allows visitors to contact the seller by telephone or WhatsApp. The website does not process card payments or automatically confirm orders.

Information you choose to send

When you contact us using WhatsApp, your message may include the product, selected model options and any roofsign or licence number you type. This information is sent through WhatsApp when you choose to send the message. WhatsApp handles communications under its own terms and privacy policy.

Website operation

The site uses necessary browser technologies to display the catalogue. During demonstration mode, product edits made in the Owner Panel are stored only in that browser. When the production database is connected, product records will be served from the configured hosting provider.
